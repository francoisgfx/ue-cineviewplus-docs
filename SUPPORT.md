# Support

Support is handled through this repository's [Issues](https://github.com/francoisgfx/ue-cineviewplus-docs/issues/new/choose).

Before opening an issue:

1. Read [Troubleshooting and FAQ](docs/09-troubleshooting.md).
2. Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/francoisgfx/ue-cineviewplus-docs/issues?q=is%3Aissue).

For a bug, please include your Unreal Engine version, operating system, GPU, steps to reproduce, and any lines mentioning `CineViewPlus` from the Output Log.

Issues are public. Do not post order details, personal information, or confidential project images.
